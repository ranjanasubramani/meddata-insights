About MedData Insights

Making healthcare information easier to understand and manage.

MedData Insights is a web-based healthcare analytics project developed to demonstrate how digital tools can support patient record management and healthcare data analysis. It brings patient records, department distribution and statistical reports together in one simple interface.

The project demonstrates the use of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Chart.js and browser-based data storage.
